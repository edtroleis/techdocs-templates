# Team rituals

How the Payments team works week to week.

## Summary

Planning on Mondays, a short daily, and a review every other Friday.

## Content

| Ritual   | When                     | How long | What for                    |
|----------|--------------------------|----------|-----------------------------|
| Planning | Monday 10:30             | 45 min   | Choose the work of the week |
| Daily    | Every day 9:45           | 15 min   | Unblock each other          |
| Review   | Every other Friday 15:00 | 30 min   | Show what shipped           |
| Retro    | Last Friday of the month | 45 min   | Improve how we work         |

Meetings are optional for whoever is on call.
