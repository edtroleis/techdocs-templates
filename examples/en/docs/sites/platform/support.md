# Support and SLAs

## How to reach the team

| For                          | Where                                  |
| ---------------------------- | -------------------------------------- |
| Questions                    | `#commerce-platform`                   |
| Requests and non-urgent bugs | A ticket in the portal, queue COMMERCE |
| Payments are failing         | Page the Payments on-call              |

## Service levels

| Capability   | Availability | First answer     |
| ------------ | ------------ | ---------------- |
| Payments     | 99.95%       | 15 minutes, 24/7 |
| Invoices     | 99.5%        | 4 business hours |
| Fraud checks | 99.0%        | 1 business day   |

## Maintenance windows

Tuesdays, 02:00 to 04:00 UTC. Planned work is posted in `#commerce-platform` two days before; payments keep
working during it.

## Status

The status page at `status.internal.example.com/commerce` shows incidents in progress and the last 90 days.
