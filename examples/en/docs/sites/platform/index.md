# Commerce Platform

What the Commerce Platform gives the teams that sell something, so none of them builds payments, invoices or
fraud checks alone.

## What it offers

| Capability   | What you get                                | Status |
| ------------ | ------------------------------------------- | ------ |
| Payments     | Card charges and refunds through one API    | Stable |
| Invoices     | Invoices issued and sent after each payment | Stable |
| Fraud checks | A risk score for every order                | Beta   |

## Where to start

1. Get access, as described in [Getting access](access.md).
2. Follow the [golden path](../../tutorials/golden-path.md) to create a service that sells.
3. Look up unfamiliar terms in the [glossary](../../reference/glossary.md).

## What it does not do

- Prices and promotions stay with each product team
- Customer support answers to customers; the platform answers to teams

## Who looks after it

The Payments team. See [Support and SLAs](support.md).
