# Getting access

## Who can use the platform

- Any team with a service in the catalog that sells to customers
- The service needs an owner and a cost center in the catalog before asking

## How to ask

1. In the portal, open **Access → Commerce Platform**.
2. Choose the service and the capabilities: Payments, Invoices, Fraud checks.
3. Sandbox access is immediate; production access is approved by the Payments team within two business days.

## What you receive

- A client for the Payments API, with its secret in the vault
- The service added to the `commerce-users` group, for the dashboards

## Removing access

When a service is retired, mark it as retired in the catalog: the access is removed the next night.
