# Roadmap

## Phases

| Phase     | What it delivers                                   | Dates           | Status      |
| --------- | -------------------------------------------------- | --------------- | ----------- |
| Discovery | The RFC approved and the contract signed           | Jul 1 – Aug 15  | Done        |
| Build     | Both providers behind one interface, 1% of traffic | Aug 16 – Oct 31 | Done        |
| Rollout   | 100% of traffic on the new provider, old one off   | Nov 1 – Dec 15  | In progress |

## Milestones

- Nov 10: 25% of payments on the new provider; Finance checks the first invoice
- Dec 1: 100% of payments; the old provider only for refunds
- Dec 15: the old contract ends

## Risks

| Risk                                          | Impact | What we do about it                |
| --------------------------------------------- | ------ | ---------------------------------- |
| Refunds of old payments need the old provider | Medium | Keep it for refunds until March    |
| Black Friday during the rollout               | High   | Freeze at 50% from Nov 20 to Dec 1 |

## Changes to this roadmap

- Oct 12: rollout moved one week later, to wait for the provider's 3-D Secure fix
