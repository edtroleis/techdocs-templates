# Onboarding: Payments team

Welcome to the Payments team. This page takes you through your first week. Ask anything in `#payments-team`:
there are no silly questions here.

## Access to request

| What                           | Where                            | Who approves |
|--------------------------------|----------------------------------|--------------|
| GitHub team `payments`         | Access portal, "GitHub teams"    | Team lead    |
| Provider dashboard (read only) | Ticket to Finance Ops            | Finance Ops  |
| Production logs                | Access portal, "Logs – payments" | Team lead    |
| On-call schedule               | PagerDuty, after your third week | Team lead    |

## Local environment

Follow [Your first payment](tutorial.md). When the test charge says `paid`, your environment is ready.

## First task

Pick an issue labeled `good first issue` in the `payments-service` repository. Pair with your buddy on the
first pull request: they will show you how we review and deploy.

## People and channels

- **Team lead:** Ana Souza
- **Your buddy:** assigned on your first day
- `#payments-team`: daily work and questions
- `#payments-alerts`: alerts (read, do not act until you are on call)
