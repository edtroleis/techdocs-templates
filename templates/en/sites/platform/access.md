## Who can use the platform

- Which teams and which kinds of service it is meant for
- What a team needs to have before asking (an owner in the catalog, a budget, a
  cost center)

## How to ask

1. Open a request in the portal (link), choosing the capabilities you need.
2. Say which service and which environments the access is for.
3. The platform team answers within the time in Support and SLAs.

## What you receive

- Access to the environments, by group, never by person
- The repositories and pipelines created for your service

## Removing access

When a service is retired or a person leaves the team, remove the access through
the same portal.
